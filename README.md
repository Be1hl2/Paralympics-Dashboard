Paralympics Dashboard Analysis
📌 Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard was developed to analyze Paralympics performance trends across countries, sports categories, and medal distributions over multiple years.
The objective of the project was to:

Evaluate country-wise medal performance
Analyze medal contribution patterns
Identify performance consistency across sports and countries
Assess participation impact on overall growth trends
Uncover hidden performance shifts through trend and contribution analysis


The dashboard enables interactive exploration of Paralympics data through KPI tracking, trend analysis, and multi-dimensional filtering.

📊 Dashboard KPIs
The dashboard includes key performance indicators to provide a consolidated overview of overall Paralympics performance:

Total Medals: 29K
Total Gold Medals: 11K
Total Silver Medals: 9K
Total Bronze Medals: 9K
Average Medals per Country: 401.89
Previous Games Growth: -83%


These KPIs help monitor overall participation, medal contribution, and historical performance trends.

🔍 Key Analysis Areas
Country-Wise Medal Performance
Analyzed Gold, Silver, and Bronze medal distribution across top-performing countries to evaluate comparative performance and contribution levels.
Trend Analysis
Evaluated year-over-year performance changes, medal share trends, and participation impact across Paralympic events.
Category & Sport-Level Analysis
Drilled into sport-wise and country-wise consistency to identify strong and declining performance areas.
Contribution & Distribution Analysis
Assessed whether total performance was driven by broader country participation or concentrated among a limited number of top-performing countries.

💡 Key Insights

Korea Republic demonstrated the strongest overall medal performance among leading countries.
Spain showed signs of declining contribution compared to other top-performing nations.
Medal distribution across Gold, Silver, and Bronze categories appeared relatively balanced.
Despite balanced distribution patterns, overall performance growth showed contraction.
Average medals per country indicated performance concentration rather than wider participation expansion.



🎯 Business Insight
Balanced distributions can sometimes conceal underlying performance decline.
The analysis revealed that reduced contribution depth and lower participation impact can significantly influence overall growth trends, even when top-level distributions appear stable.
This highlights the importance of evaluating:
contribution consistency
participation spread
country-level dependency
long-term performance sustainability
rather than relying only on aggregate totals.
